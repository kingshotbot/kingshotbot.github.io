Set up in a few minutes

  1. Install BlueStacks 5 and Kingshot inside it.
  2. In BlueStacks, open Settings, then Advanced, and turn on Android Debug Bridge.
  3. Download Phantom and run the installer. No Python or other tools are needed.
  4. Open Phantom, add your BlueStacks instance and press Start.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) · 8 GB of memory, 16 GB recommended

Questions

Can I still play myself?

Yes. Press F9 to pause and play, F10 to hand it back.

Will it spend my gems?

No. Anything that spends gems, keys or speed-ups is off until you turn it on, and always has a limit you choose.

What if the game updates?

Phantom downloads the game's new resources when asked and keeps going.

Something looks wrong. What do I do?

Use Report in the tray menu. Phantom shows exactly which logs and screenshots go into the report and saves it as a zip in a folder you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere; share the zip with us on Discord.

How do I get help?

Join the Phantom Discord server.
