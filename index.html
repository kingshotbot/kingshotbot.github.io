It plays the real game, the way you do.

Reads the screen, then taps

Phantom looks at Kingshot running in BlueStacks and taps what a player would tap. It never modifies the game.

Never spends your gems

It never spends gems and never buys offers. Anything that uses keys stays off until you turn it on.

You stay in charge

Pause any account to play yourself, then press Start to hand it back. Phantom lives in the tray, sends notifications and keeps an Activity history of what it did.

Help when something looks off

Report packs the logs and screenshots in one click. You see exactly what is in it before it goes to us for support.

Speaks your language

The app comes in 10 languages.

Set up in a few minutes.

  1. Install BlueStacks 5 and Kingshot.Kingshot goes inside BlueStacks.
  2. Turn on Android Debug Bridge.In BlueStacks, open Settings, then Advanced.
  3. Download Phantom and run the installer.If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", see the first question below.
  4. Open Phantom, add your BlueStacks instance and press Start.Keep the PC awake while it plays.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  • BlueStacks 5 with Kingshot installed
  • 8 GB of memory, 16 GB recommended
  • Internet when you first start the trial
  • No Python or other tools

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". What do I do?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Download it only from the button on this page.

Is it free?

You start with a free trial: 15 days from your first run, with all the Pro features for 3 hours a day. No card or account needed. After that, Free covers daily rewards and chores for 1 hour a day. Pro runs every feature all day, from $8.49 a month. See Plans.

How do I buy a plan?

Join the Discord and ask. You get a licence key to paste on the app's Plan page.

Can I still play myself?

Yes. Press Pause on the account in Phantom, play, then press Start to hand it back.

Will it spend my gems?

No. Phantom never spends gems and never buys offers. Anything that uses keys stays off until you turn it on.

What if the game updates?

Phantom downloads the game's new resources when asked and keeps going.

Something looks wrong. What do I do?

Use Report in the app. Phantom shows exactly which logs and screenshots go into the report and saves it as a zip in a folder you choose. Share the zip with us on Discord.

How do I get help?

Join the Phantom Discord server.
